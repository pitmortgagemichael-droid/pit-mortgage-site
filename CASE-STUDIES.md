# Adding Michael's real cases

The case section stays hidden until approved stories are added to `cases.js`.

For each story collect:
- Client-approved, anonymised photo or document excerpt (store under `assets/cases/`).
- Situation: what the client wanted and the obstacle.
- Approach: what Michael helped prepare, compare or resolve.
- Outcome: documented result, including relevant costs and conditions.
- Case date and permission to publish.

Remove names, IC/passport numbers, addresses, account numbers, signatures, QR codes and other identifying details from the actual image file before committing. Do not merely cover them with CSS. Do not commit original private documents to this public repository. No invented testimonials or approval claims.

Add objects matching the schema noted in cases.js. The section becomes visible automatically when at least one story exists.
